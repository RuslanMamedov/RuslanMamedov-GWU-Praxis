**Key References for Hybrid Causal Semi-Supervised VAE with Anchored Latents, Residual Blocks, Causal Layers/SCMs, and Empirical Validation on Interventional Data**

### Foundational CausalVAE and SCM-Integrated VAEs
- **Yang, M., Liu, F., Chen, Z., Shen, X., Hao, J., & Wang, J. (2021). CausalVAE: Disentangled Representation Learning via Neural Structural Causal Models. CVPR.**  
  Introduces the core CausalVAE with a Causal Layer (mask-based linear SCM) transforming independent exogenous noise into causally related endogenous latents. Supports do-operations and counterfactuals; analyzes identifiability with supervision. Directly foundational for your structured latent + causal DAG layer.

- **Komanduri, A., Wu, Y., et al. (2022). SCM-VAE: Learning Identifiable Causal Representations via Structural Knowledge. IEEE Big Data.**  
  Direct extension of CausalVAE ideas; incorporates a priori causal knowledge, structural causal prior, and non-linear additive noise SCM in latent space for improved identifiability and independent causal mechanisms. Explicit SCM on latents.

### Hybrid/Anchored Causal VAEs (Closest to Your Semi-Supervised Anchored + Residual Design)
- **Liu, E., et al. (2025). Learning Genetic Perturbation Effects with Variational Causal Models (SCCVAE / Single Cell Causal VAE). bioRxiv / PLOS Computational Biology.**  
  Hybrid mechanistic SCM + VAE for single-cell perturbations. Integrates gene network knowledge (anchored causal structure) with variational inference; latent space for functional modules and extrapolation to unseen perturbations. Strong match for anchored known variables + residual mechanisms in perturbation data.

- **Al-Ali, S., et al. (2026). Multi-Channel Causal Variational Autoencoder (MC²VAE) for Multimodal Biomedical Causal Disentanglement.**  
  Multi-channel causal VAE for joint modality-specific latents and causal inference; relevant for imaging + other high-dim data.

### Related Causal Latent Models and Counterfactual VAEs
- **Pawlowski, N., et al. (2020). Deep Structural Causal Models for Tractable Counterfactual Inference. NeurIPS.**  
  General framework for deep SCMs (including VAE-compatible components) enabling tractable interventions and counterfactuals.

- **Fan, D., et al. (2023/2024). Causal Flows Variational Autoencoders (CauF-VAE).**  
  VAE with causal flows for causally disentangled representations.

- **Other extensions**: Works on latent SCM inference, counterfactual generative modeling with variational causal inference (e.g., Wu et al.), and disentangled causal effect VAEs (DCEVAE) for interventions/counterfactuals.

### Datasets & Empirical Validation Contexts
- **Gamella, J.L., Peters, J., & Bühlmann, P. (2024/2025). The Causal Chambers: Real Physical Systems as a Testbed for AI Methodology. arXiv / Nature Machine Intelligence.**  
  Open hardware with designed interventions, ground-truth DAGs, image/sensor data — ideal for rigorous L2/L3 validation of your model.

- **Chandrasekaran, S.N., et al. (2024). Three million images and morphological profiles... JUMP Cell Painting (CPJUMP1). Nature Methods.**  
  Large-scale chemical/genetic perturbations with matched pairs; high-dim imaging for anchored interventions and hidden mechanism discovery.

### Additional Relevant Works
- Papers on causal representation learning in imaging (e.g., brain MRI counterfactuals, deep causality VAE for biomarkers).
- Surveys on causal generative modeling (e.g., Akomand GitHub survey covering CausalVAE + SCM-VAE).

These works provide strong precedents for your hybrid architecture (anchored supervised blocks + residual + learnable sparse causal DAG layer) and emphasize empirical validation via interventions.
